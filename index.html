Good to know

Stretches should feel like gentle tension, never pain. Stop if you feel tingling, numbness, dizziness or sharp pain, and see a physio or doctor if neck pain lasts or spreads down your arm.

The posture part is the best-supported: chin tucks (deep neck flexor training) plus chest stretches are the standard core of forward-head-posture programmes. The "lymphatic" part is honest movement: breathing and calf work genuinely move fluid, but no 3-minute routine "detoxes" you. Its real value is waking the body up.
